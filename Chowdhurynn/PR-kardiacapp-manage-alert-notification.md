    - Created new procedure: `p_kardiacapp-understanding-ecg-analysis`
    - Procedure's Goal: Interpret Kardia ECG analysis results and troubleshoot unreadable recordings to ensure accurate heart rhythm monitoring.

## User Scenario

James is a 55-year-old working professional diagnosed with arrhythmia who uses his Kardia app for daily ECG monitoring. One morning, after taking his routine ECG recording, he receives an "Unreadable" result instead of his usual normal reading. Concerned that he might be missing important heart rhythm information, James needs to understand what caused the unreadable result and how to resolve it for future recordings. His goal is to access his ECG history to review the unreadable recording, learn what factors cause poor recording quality, apply troubleshooting techniques to improve his recordings, and determine whether he needs to contact his doctor about the failed reading. James is tech-comfortable and systematic about problem-solving, but gets frustrated when he can't get clear readings and worries that technical issues might prevent him from detecting actual heart problems.
